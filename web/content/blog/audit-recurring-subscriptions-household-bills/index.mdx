When households try to cut spending, they almost always start in the hardest possible place: trying to spend less on weekly groceries or cutting out their morning coffee. Meanwhile, hundreds of dollars a month leak out silently through forgotten app trials, duplicate streaming tiers, and the "loyalty tax" on auto-renewed insurance, electricity, and internet plans.

Because recurring charges are automated across debit cards, credit cards, PayPal, and Apple/Google app stores, a 30-minute recurring bill audit gives you permanent monthly savings without changing your daily lifestyle at all.

## Step 1: Surface Every Recurring Charge Across the Last 90 Days

Many subscriptions bill quarterly or annually, and others hide behind payment aggregators (`APPLE.COM/BILL`, `GOOGLE*`, `PAYPAL *`).

To catch all of them at once:
1. Upload your last three months of PDF or CSV statements using PFinance's [Bank Statement Import](/features/bank-statement-import/).
2. Run the **Subscription & Recurring Transaction Detector** in PFinance, which groups repeat merchants by billing cadence (weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly, and annual) and flags price increases.

## Step 2: Sort Recurring Charges into Three Buckets

Go through your recurring list and assign every item to one of three actions:

| Bucket | Examples | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Cut Immediately** | Unused streaming services, duplicate cloud storage, free trials that converted, gym memberships unused for 60+ days | Cancel today and set a reminder before any annual renewal |
| **2. Rotate or Downgrade** | 4 simultaneous video streaming services, premium family tiers for 2 people, high-data mobile plans | Keep 1–2 active at a time; drop to the tier you actually use |
| **3. Benchmark & Renegotiate (The Loyalty Tax)** | Electricity, gas, NBN internet, car insurance, home & contents insurance, mortgage rate | Compare against new-customer market rates once a year |

<Callout type="tip">
Cancelling two $22/month streaming services and saving $45/month on your NBN and mobile plans frees up **$1,068 per year** of after-tax cash—equivalent to a ~$1,600 pre-tax pay rise once you account for income tax and Medicare levy in our [Australian Salary Calculator](/tools/australian-salary-calculator/).
</Callout>

## Step 3: Beat the Australian Loyalty Tax on Major Bills

In Australia, energy retailers, insurers, and telcos routinely reserve their best discounts for new customers while ratcheting up prices on existing customers at every renewal notice:

### Electricity and Gas
- Check your latest electricity bill for the retailer's mandatory statement on the front page telling you whether they can offer you a cheaper plan.
- Compare your supply charge (c/day) and usage tariff (c/kWh) on the government's free **Energy Made Easy** (or **Victorian Energy Compare** in VIC) benchmark site.

### Car and Home Insurance
- Never let an annual insurance policy auto-renew without running a fresh online quote with the same insurer as a "new customer."
- Often, the exact same insurer will quote 15–25% less online for a new policy than the renewal notice they just emailed you.

### Mobile and NBN Internet
- Check your actual monthly gigabyte usage on your mobile bill. If you use 18GB/month on a $75/month 150GB plan, switching to a $30–$35 MVNO plan on the same network saves nearly $500/year per person.
- Remember: if you claim a work-related percentage of your mobile or internet bill in the [Tax Deduction Tracker](/features/tax-deductions/), store the updated invoice with the [Receipt Scanner](/features/receipt-scanner/).

Savings from a bill audit disappear into general spending unless you give them a job on the same day:

1. Plug your new lower fixed-cost numbers into the [Interactive Budget Calculator](/tools/budget-calculator/).
2. If you share bills with a partner or housemates, update your recurring items in [Household Budgeting](/features/household-budgeting/) so everyone sees the lower monthly commitment.
3. Redirect the monthly difference into your mortgage offset account, emergency fund, or voluntary contributions modeled in the [Super Calculator](/tools/super-calculator/).
