Most "AI" features in consumer finance apps are little more than a chatbot wrapper that repeats your account balance back to you—or worse, an opaque black box that miscategorizes transactions without asking.

When we designed **PFinance Assistant**, we built it around two strict engineering principles:

1. **Proactive multi-tool investigation**: When you ask a question like *"How are my finances looking this month?"*, the assistant doesn't ask if you want it to check—it autonomously queries your spending summary, active budget progress, income streams, savings goals, and upcoming bills in parallel to synthesize a complete answer.
2. **Draft-first human confirmation for every change**: Any action that creates, updates, deletes, or reclassifies a financial record runs in preview mode (`confirmed=false`) first, showing you an interactive confirmation card with duplicate warnings before a single cent is written to your ledger.

### 1. Audit Forgotten Subscriptions and Upcoming Bills

Ask: *"What bills do I have coming up in the next 30 days, and are there any recurring subscriptions I should review?"*

The assistant calls `get_upcoming_bills` and `detect_subscriptions` together—listing every scheduled bill due before next month alongside recurring card charges detected over the last six months (even if you haven't formally added them as recurring transactions yet). It is the same audit our [recurring subscriptions and bills guide](/blog/audit-recurring-subscriptions-household-bills/) walks through manually, compressed into one prompt.

### 2. Check Shared Household Group Balances

Ask: *"How much do I owe in our Household group this month?"*

Instead of opening a spreadsheet, the assistant calls `get_household_groups` to pull total shared spending, unsettled expense counts, and each member's total paid, total owed, and net balance from your [Household Budgeting](/features/household-budgeting/) workspace.

### 3. Teach PFinance Permanent Merchant Rules

Ask: *"Always categorize 'Sydney Water' as Utilities."*

When you import PDF or CSV statements via [Bank Statement Import](/features/bank-statement-import/), merchant normalization cleans up raw bank strings. Telling the assistant to save a merchant category rule previews the mapping (`"sydney water" → UTILITIES`) and, once confirmed, applies it automatically to every future statement upload.

### 4. Stress-Test Upcoming Purchases With Cash-Flow Forecasting

Ask: *"Based on my cash-flow forecast for the next 60 days, can I afford a $2,400 flight without missing my emergency fund goal?"*

For Pro users, the assistant combines `get_cash_flow_forecast`, `get_budget_progress`, and `list_goals` to compare your projected net surplus over the next 60 days against your active savings targets — the same forward view our [cash flow forecasting and sinking funds guide](/blog/cash-flow-forecasting-sinking-funds-upcoming-bills-australia/) builds manually.

### 5. Run a Pre-EOFY Tax Deduction Sweep

Ask: *"I work as a software engineer—scan my expenses this financial year for potential tax deductions."*

The assistant calls `find_potential_deductions` and `get_tax_summary`, surfacing unclassified expenses that match Australian Taxation Office categories (`D1` through `D15`) and letting you batch-confirm work-related claims in the [Tax Deduction Tracker](/features/tax-deductions/).
